# Workflow improvement discovered during an issue

## Setup
A run exposes a generic repeatable weakness in the workflow.

## Must
- Allow recurrence across independent issues/features to strengthen proposal evidence without auto-promoting or auto-adopting behavior.
- Finish or continue the issue without silently editing the canonical workflow.
- Create/present a structured improvement proposal.
- Classify generic vs project-specific vs adapter-specific.
- Require human approval before adoption.
