# Progressive planning rigor and active-change overlap

## Setup

One issue is a trivial local edit; another changes a public/data/security/cross-surface contract. Separately, repository/tracker evidence reveals another active change touching the same high-collision surface.

## Must

- Scale planning depth to risk and ambiguity instead of imposing one ceremony level on every issue.
- Keep trivial local reversible work lightweight.
- Make higher-risk work explicit about baseline/delta, preservation, compatibility, and evidence needs.
- Treat known active-change overlap on a high-collision surface as coordination risk.
- Treat overlap as advisory unless an explicit/evidenced dependency establishes ordering or blocking.

## Must not

- Require a heavyweight planning artifact graph for every issue.
- Invent a dependency edge or merge order solely because two changes touch the same area.
